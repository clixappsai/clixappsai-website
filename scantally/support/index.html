ScanTally turns paper into something you can use: a clean PDF you can search, or a receipt read into a row you can total and export. Everything happens on your phone.

Getting a good scan

  • Hold the phone flat over the page. The reading at the top shows how many degrees off parallel you are, and the shot fires on its own once you are square and steady.
  • In a hurry, or scanning something awkward? Switch to Manual and press the shutter yourself.
  • If the edges land in the wrong place, tap the page after the scan and drag the corners. A magnifier follows your finger so you can put them exactly on the paper.
  • Hold a page to move or remove it before saving.
  • Clean, Colour and Grey change how the page is finished. Clean is right for most paper.

Receipts and what gets read

  • Scan with Receipt and ScanTally reads the merchant, the date, the total, the tax and the currency.
  • Extraction is a best effort on paper that is often creased, faded or oddly printed. Anything it gets wrong you can correct — tap the details under a saved receipt and edit any field.
  • To change a category quickly, hold a receipt in the list and pick a new one. Hold several at once after tapping Select.

Getting the table out

  • Under Spend, tap Export as a table. Whatever the filters are showing is what comes out.
  • Narrow first if you want a period: the filter menu has this month, this quarter, this year and this tax year, which begins where your country's tax year actually begins.
  • Four shapes: CSV for accountants and bookkeeping software, Excel when dates and amounts need to stay dates and amounts, one PDF of every page, or a zip holding the summary and every scan together.
  • To pick particular ones instead, tap Select beside Library.

Bringing in what you already have

  • Import takes an image or a PDF you already have, from your files or your saved images, and asks whether it is a document or a receipt.
  • Convert turns a Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, Numbers, web page, text or CSV file into a PDF — and can pull the text or a printed table back out of one.
  • Sharing a file to ScanTally from another app does the same thing.

Finding something later

Search covers names, merchants and every word on every page you have scanned, and a result shows you the line it matched. Scans can also be found from your phone's own search without opening the app — there is a switch for that in settings.

Your files are yours

Scans are kept in a ScanTally folder in your phone's files. Open it, copy it out, back it up — nothing is locked in, and there is no account to lose access to. Exported PDFs are searchable in whatever opens them, because the words are laid into the file.

Common questions

Does anything leave my phone? No. There is no account, no server and no internet code in the app. See the privacy policy.

A total came out wrong. Tap the details under the receipt and correct it. The totals and any export follow immediately.

A page is crooked or cut off. Tap the page in the review screen and drag the corners, or scan it again — holding the phone flatter usually fixes it on its own.

Pulling a table out of a scan is missing. That one needs a newer phone than yours; the rest of the app does not.

How do I delete everything? Delete items inside the app, or delete the app — it all lives on your phone.

Still stuck?

Email appshelp@clixappsai.com and tell us what you were doing and what happened. If it involves a particular scan, describing the paper helps more than you would think.
