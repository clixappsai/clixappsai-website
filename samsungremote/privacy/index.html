Remote for Samsung TV – Clix

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy covers Remote for Samsung TV – Clix, published by ClixAppsAi on the Apple App Store (“the app”, “we”, “us”). The app is a remote control for Samsung Smart TVs: it runs on your iPhone and controls your TV directly over your own home Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

No account, no sign-up, no ads. The app does not ask you to register, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or location.

1. Information the app handles

  • Your TVs — stored only on your phone. So you don’t have to pair again, the app remembers each TV you connect: its network address, its MAC address (read from the TV and used only to switch it on), the pairing key the TV issues, a fingerprint of the TV’s security certificate, and any name you give the TV. This stays on your device (pairing keys in the iOS Keychain) and is never sent to us. “Forget” on a TV, or deleting the app, removes it.
  • Local network access. iOS asks your permission for the app to use your local network. The app uses it only to find your TV and send it remote-control commands; nothing on your network is collected.
  • Your free trial and purchase. The one-time unlock is sold through Apple using the App Store’s own in-app purchase system; we never see your payment details. The date your 3-day trial started is kept in your iCloud Keychain so it stays the same if you reinstall.
  • Anonymous usage record. So we can see whether the app actually works for people and fix what doesn’t, the app keeps one anonymous record per installation in Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit). It holds facts such as: the app version; whether a search found a TV; whether pairing succeeded and, if not, the error the TV gave; your TV’s model name and connection type; whether your phone was on Wi-Fi or mobile data; whether a help screen was opened; and your trial and purchase status. It is identified only by a random ID the app creates — not linked to your name, email, Apple ID details or device identity — and is never used for advertising, tracking or sold to anyone.

The app contains no advertising and no third-party SDKs of any kind — it uses Apple’s own services only.

2. How your information is used

  • To remember your TVs, reconnect to them, and switch them on.
  • To run the free trial and unlock the full remote after a one-time purchase.
  • To understand how well the app works — for example how often people manage to connect a TV — so we can fix what isn’t working.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

3. How information is shared

  • Apple — app distribution, in-app purchase, and iCloud (CloudKit) storage of the anonymous usage record. Apple processes this under its own privacy policy.
  • Your TV. The app talks directly to your TV on your local network. Your remote-control commands are not routed through our servers — we don’t have any.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Data storage and security

Information about your TVs is stored on your device, with pairing keys in the iOS Keychain. The connection to your TV is encrypted where the TV supports it (2018 and newer Samsung TVs). The anonymous usage record is sent to Apple’s iCloud over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your TV details live on your device, so you are in control: use Forget on a saved TV, or delete the app. The anonymous usage record carries no personal details; if you would like it removed, email us and we will help. Purchase records are held by Apple under its own policies.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
