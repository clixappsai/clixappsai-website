WatchMinds: Film & Series Picks

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 June 2026

WatchMinds (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a taste-based film and series recommendation app published by ClixAppsAI. It learns the kinds of stories you’re drawn to and suggests things to watch. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using WatchMinds you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Account information. Your email address and password, used to create your account and sign you in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form — we never see your plain password.
  • Your taste profile. The answers you give during onboarding, the taste profile we derive from them, your reactions to recommendations (loved / seen it / not for me), and your watchlist. This is what powers your picks.
  • Viewing preferences. Optional details that make picks more relevant — your region, the streaming services you use, your languages, and an age bracket.
  • Notification token. If you turn on notifications, a device push token so we can send your daily picks reminder.
  • Basic technical data. Standard information needed to deliver the service securely (for example, request and connection data handled by our hosting provider). The app does not include advertising or third-party tracking/analytics SDKs.

2. The identity firewall

WatchMinds is built so that your taste is kept separate from your identity. Your taste profile is stored against an anonymous identifier — not your name or email — and the AI that builds your profile and ranks your picks only ever receives anonymous taste signals, never your email or who you are. We never attach a name to a taste profile.

3. How we use your information

  • To build your taste profile and generate personalised film and series recommendations.
  • To improve those recommendations over time as you react to what we suggest.
  • To send service emails you trigger (sign-up and password-reset codes) and, if you opt in, a daily picks notification.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How information is shared

  • With service providers who operate the app on our behalf:
    • Supabase — cloud database and authentication that hold your account and taste data.
    • Anthropic (Claude) — the AI that turns your taste signals into a profile and ranks your picks. It receives anonymous taste signals only — never your identity.
    • The Movie Database (TMDB) — the source of film and series information (titles, posters, details). We look up content from TMDB; we do not send your personal data to them.
    • Resend — delivery of the sign-up and password-reset emails described above.
    • Expo & Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivery of push notifications, if you enable them.
    • Apple & Google — app distribution via their app stores.
    These providers process data only to provide their service to us.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on our providers’ servers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app, which removes your login and your entire taste profile. You can also request deletion by email — see Delete account.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. You can delete everything directly in the app, or contact us and we will help.

8. Children’s privacy

WatchMinds is intended for teenagers and adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, with an in-app notice.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email appshelp@clixappsai.com.
