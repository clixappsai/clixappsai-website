Frequently asked questions

How does WatchMinds decide what to recommend?

It asks you a few quick questions about how stories make you feel — not genres. From your answers it builds a taste profile and suggests films and series that fit it, getting sharper as you react to what it shows you.

How do I get my picks, and more of them?

Your picks appear on the home screen. Swipe through them, and tap Load more for the next set. A fresh batch lands each day.

What do the swipes do?

Swipe ← for “not for me”, ↑ for “seen it”, and → to add a title to your Watchlist. Your reactions teach the app what you like, so the picks keep improving.

Why do I run out of picks for the day?

You get a set number of fresh recommendations each day. Once you’ve been through them, a new set lands tomorrow — your Watchlist is always available in the meantime.

Can I re-do the taste questions?

Yes. Go to Settings → Taste profile and retake — it rebuilds your profile from your new answers.

The app’s colour changed — is that a bug?

No — that’s intentional. The app’s colour is a live expression of your taste, so it shifts as your profile does.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?. We’ll email you an 8-digit code — enter it along with your new password. If the email doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder.

I signed in but my profile is missing.

This usually means a different email was used. Make sure you sign in with the exact email you created your account with, using Log in (not Create account).

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and your entire taste profile. See Delete account for details.

How much does WatchMinds cost?

WatchMinds is free to use, with no ads.
