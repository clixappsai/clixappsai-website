Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Vigful is built to be private by design. It runs almost entirely on your phone: it detects charge zones, reads your documents, and reminds you about bills, renewals and medication without accounts, without a login, and without sending your personal data to us or anyone else. We don’t run servers that hold your information, we don’t track you, and we don’t show ads.

This policy explains, plainly, what data Vigful handles and where it stays.

The short version

  • Your data lives on your phone (and, only if you turn on cloud sync, in your own private cloud account).
  • We never receive your location, your documents, your medication, your reminders, or your vehicle details.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no selling of data.

Location

  • Vigful uses your location only to detect when you enter or are inside a chargeable or restricted driving zone (for example an airport drop-off, a clean-air zone, London’s ULEZ, or an Italian ZTL).
  • All of this detection happens on your device. Your location is never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • We ask for “Always” location so detection can work in the background, even when the app is closed — that is the only way we can warn you before you’re fined. You can change or revoke this at any time in your phone’s Settings → Location / Privacy → Vigful. If you turn it off, background zone detection simply stops working; nothing is sent anywhere either way.

Camera and photos

  • If you snap a bill, letter, parking notice, or a screenshot to capture a reminder, Vigful reads the text using on-device text recognition and on-device AI. The image and the text are processed on your phone and are not uploaded.
  • Vigful only accesses a photo you explicitly choose or a picture you take; it does not browse your photo library.

The things you add

Reminders, bills, subscriptions, renewals, medication details, and any vehicle information (such as a registration number, MOT or tax dates) that you enter or that Vigful reads for you are stored on your device. This includes health-related information such as medication names, doses and schedules. Vigful keeps this only to power your reminders. It is not transmitted to us.

Cloud sync (optional)

If you turn on cloud backup/sync in Settings, your Vigful data is synchronised through your own private cloud account provided by your device’s platform — Apple’s iCloud on iPhone, or your Google account on Android. This is between your devices and that provider — we have no access to it, and the provider’s handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy. You can turn this off at any time, and manage or delete the synced data via your phone’s account/cloud settings.

Notifications

Vigful uses local notifications to remind you about a charge, a renewal, or a dose. These are scheduled and delivered on your device. We do not run a push-notification server and do not collect notification data.

Charge-zone data

To tell you where the zones are, Vigful downloads a small, read-only zone data file for your selected driving country from a static hosted file. This is a normal file download — no personal data, location, or identifier is sent with it, and it is the same file for every user.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not create user profiles or sell, rent, or share your data.
  • We do not have accounts, so we hold no email, password, or profile for you.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you are in control. Delete any item inside the app, or delete the Vigful app, to remove its data from your phone. If you use cloud sync, remove Vigful’s synced data via your phone’s account/cloud settings.

Children

Vigful is a general-purpose driving and reminder utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children (and, in fact, we don’t collect personal information from anyone on our side).

Medical disclaimer

Vigful’s medication features are a reminder aid, not medical advice. Always follow your prescription and the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist, and confirm anything Vigful reads from a label.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Vigful handles data — for example, if a future optional feature (such as an official DVLA vehicle look-up) requires sending your vehicle registration to a service on your behalf — we will update this policy and describe it clearly here before that feature is enabled.
