webOS TV Remote · LG TV Remote

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This policy covers the remote-control app published by ClixAppsAi — listed as webOS TV Remote on the Apple App Store and as LG TV Remote on Google Play (“the app”, “we”, “us”). It is the same app on both platforms: a remote control for webOS Smart TVs that runs on your phone and controls your TV over your own local Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

No account, no sign-up. The app does not ask you to register, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

1. Information the app handles

  • Your TVs — stored only on your device. The TVs you connect to are remembered on your phone so you don’t have to pair again: the TV’s IP address, its MAC address (detected from the TV itself, or entered by you, and used only to switch the TV on), the pairing key the TV issues, and any name you give a TV. This information stays in the app’s local storage on your device and is never sent to us. Removing a TV with “Forget”, clearing the app’s storage, or uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Purchase status. To run the free trial and the one-time unlock, we use RevenueCat. It processes an anonymous, app-generated identifier and your purchase / entitlement status. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Anonymous usage events. So we can see whether the app is actually working for people and improve it, a small number of events are recorded against that same anonymous identifier through RevenueCat — for example whether you have successfully connected a TV and on what date, whether a search for TVs found one, whether your free trial has ended, and your TV’s model name and features. These describe how the app is used, not who you are: they are not linked to your name, email, or device identity, and are never used for advertising or sold to anyone.
  • Voice control. When you tap the microphone, your device’s own built-in speech recognition (provided by Apple on iPhone and iPad, or Google on Android) converts your speech to text. That audio is handled by the speech service on your device; the app receives only the resulting text and acts on it. The app itself does not record, store, or transmit audio.

The app contains no advertising and no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. How your information is used

  • To remember your TVs and reconnect to them.
  • To provide the free trial and unlock premium features after a one-time purchase.
  • To understand how well the app works — for example how often people manage to connect a TV — so we can fix what is not working and improve it.
  • To carry out the voice commands you speak.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How information is shared

  • Service providers who help operate the app:
    • RevenueCat — manages the trial and one-time purchase, and receives the anonymous usage events described above.
    • Apple / Google — the device’s own speech-recognition service that converts your voice to text.
    • Apple & Google — app distribution and in-app purchases via their app stores.
    These providers process data only to provide their service to us.
  • Your TV. The app communicates directly with your TV on your local network. Your control of the TV is not routed through our servers.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Data storage and security

Information about your TVs is stored locally on your device. Data sent to the providers above is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because your TV details live on your device, you are in control: use Forget on a saved TV, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app to remove them. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and the app stores are retained under their own policies. Questions about deletion? Email us and we will help.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email appshelp@clixappsai.com.
