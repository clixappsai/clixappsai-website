SplitsRight: Expense Splitter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

SplitsRight (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a shared expense-splitting app published by ClixAppsAI. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using SplitsRight you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Account information. Your email address and password, used to create your account and sign you in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form — we never see your plain password.
  • Content you create. The groups you create or join, the names and email addresses of people you add as group members, expenses (amounts, categories, dates, notes), payments/settlements, and recurring entries.
  • Photos you choose to add. Optional receipt photos and optional proof-of-payment photos that you attach to an expense or settlement.
  • Basic technical data. Standard information needed to deliver the service securely (for example, request and connection data handled by our hosting provider). The app does not include advertising or third-party tracking/analytics SDKs.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: storing your groups, expenses and balances and syncing them across your devices and the other members of your groups.
  • To send service emails you trigger — group invitations, “who owes whom” balance summaries, settlement notifications, and password-reset codes.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How information is shared

  • With other members of your groups. SplitsRight is collaborative: people in a group can see that group’s shared content — including your display name, the email you’re known by in the group, the expenses and payments, and any photos attached to them. Only add people you intend to share that information with.
  • With service providers who operate the app on our behalf:
    • Supabase — cloud database, authentication and file storage that holds your account and group data.
    • Resend — delivery of the transactional emails described above.
    • Apple — app distribution via the App Store.
    These providers process data only to provide their service to us.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on our provider’s servers protected by row-level security so that only members of a group can access that group’s data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. When you delete your account, your login is removed and your personal identity is detached from past shared activity (your past entries remain in shared groups as “Former member” so other members’ balances stay correct). If you would like us to help with a data request, contact us at the address below.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. You can exercise most of these directly in the app, or contact us and we will help.

7. Children’s privacy

SplitsRight is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, with an in-app notice.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email appshelp@clixappsai.com.
