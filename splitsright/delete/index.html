SplitsRight: Expense Splitter

Delete account

You can delete your SplitsRight account and personal data at any time — directly in the app, or by request below.

Request deletion by email

Email us from your sign-in address. Handled within 7 days, always within 30.

appshelp@clixappsai.com

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

Open SplitsRight → Settings → Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2 — By email

Email appshelp@clixappsai.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject “Delete my SplitsRight account.” We verify ownership of that address and delete the account — normally within 7 days and always within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your personal profile data — name and email address.
  • Receipt and proof-of-payment photos you uploaded.

What is kept (anonymized)

So other people’s balances stay correct, the expenses and payments you recorded inside shared groups aren’t removed — instead your name is replaced with “Former member,” so your personal identity is no longer associated with them.
